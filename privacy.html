Privacy Policy

AbInt is a personal application built and used by a single individual. It is not distributed to, or used by, anyone else.

AbInt accesses Google Calendar data solely to read, create, update, and delete calendar events on behalf of its single authorized user, at that user's direct request. All data stays on that user's own hardware. No data is sold, shared, transmitted to any third party, or used for advertising or analytics of any kind.

Since this application has exactly one user who is also its developer, questions about this policy have no one else to be directed to.
